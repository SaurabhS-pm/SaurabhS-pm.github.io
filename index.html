Case studies

Three problems I've taken apart.

01

PaymentIntelligenceOS: mapping merchant payment stacks from public pages

Payments
Context

Payments teams guess at what a prospect already runs. Stack-detection tools flatten everything into one verdict, so you can't tell what was observed from what was assumed.

What I did

Built a crawler over public pages only — 50 merchants, five countries, 170 signatures across 61 vendors — recording sighted, possible and inferred as separate states.

Outcome

Found a third-party checkout vendor sitting between store and PSP on half the Indian sample, a layer absent across the other four countries.

03

A voice AI agent for payment support

Voice AI
Context

Declined cards, disputed charges and forgotten subscriptions still arrive by phone, where the hold time often costs more than the transaction.

What I'm building

A voice agent that resolves the routine cases end to end and hands off with full context when it can't, scoped to the calls where a wrong answer is cheap to correct.

How it gets judged

Containment rate as the primary metric, with handoff quality as the guardrail: a bad escalation counts as a failure, not a resolution.
