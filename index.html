Mapping merchant payment stacks from public pages

ProblemPayments teams guess at what a prospect already runs. Stack-detection tools flatten everything into one verdict, so you can't tell what was observed from what was assumed.

SolutionCrawls public merchant pages and maps the payment stack behind them — 50 merchants, five countries, 170 signatures across 61 vendors — reporting what was sighted separately from what was inferred.

OutcomeFound a third-party checkout vendor sitting between store and PSP on half the Indian sample, a layer absent across the other four countries.

03

Competitive Intelligence RAG

RAG Completed

Answering product questions from a competitor's reviews

ProblemPMs monitor competitors by reading reviews, which does not scale. A store's rating summary gives a number and no reason; a listening tool is priced for a marketing team, not a PM with a question on a Tuesday.

SolutionPulls 500 reviews per app from both stores, embeds them locally, and answers a plain-English product question from the retrieved ones with a sentiment breakdown attached.

OutcomeA confidence threshold showed five to ten percent of reviews are genuinely ambiguous, so the breakdown reports them as uncertain rather than implying precision the data cannot support.
